# DoRev: AI-Powered Google Review Manager 🚀

## 💡 Introduction

**DoRev** offers businesses a smart, automated way to manage Google reviews. By scanning a QR code, customers can easily share their feedback — which is then analyzed, categorized, and filtered using AI and NLP technologies. DoRev ensures high-quality reviews are highlighted and harmful ones are filtered, preserving brand reputation and saving time.

## 🎯 Objectives

- **Improve Efficiency**: Automate analysis and classification of customer reviews using NLP-based sentiment analysis.
- **Enhance Review Quality**: Publish only positive and constructive feedback using a sentiment analysis model.
- **Deliver Insights**: Visualize customer sentiment and analytics via an intuitive dashboard.
- **Protect Reputation**: Detect and filter toxic reviews using NLP-based toxicity detection models.

## 📚 Literature Survey & Limitations of Existing Systems

Existing systems often struggle with:
- **Manual Handling**: Time-consuming and error-prone review management.
- **Poor Sentiment Interpretation**: Inaccurate tone and context analysis.
- **Inconsistent Quality**: Harmful or irrelevant reviews often remain visible.
- **Lack of Analytics**: Limited insights from customer feedback.

DoRev addresses these gaps with a fully AI-powered solution.

## ❓ Problem Statement

Businesses face challenges in managing a large volume of Google reviews, especially identifying valuable feedback and filtering toxic content. Manual systems are inefficient, and many tools lack advanced sentiment analysis or dashboard visualization. A comprehensive, automated solution is required to streamline review management and enable data-driven decisions.

## 🧠 System Design

DoRev is structured around four key components:
1. **Review Collection** via QR Code
2. **AI-Powered Sentiment Analysis**
3. **Toxicity Filtering**
4. **Analytics Dashboard** for actionable insights
